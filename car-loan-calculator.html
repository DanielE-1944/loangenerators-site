What a residual payment does

A residual (balloon) payment is a lump sum you owe when the loan ends, usually set near the car's expected value at that point. Because you are not paying it off during the term, the monthly payment is lower. The trade-off is that the unpaid part keeps accruing interest the whole time, and at the end you must pay it, refinance it, or sell the car.

Worked example: $25,000 at 7% over 60 months

With no residual, the payment is $495.03 a month, interest is $4,702, and you pay $29,702 in total. With a $5,000 residual, the payment drops to $425.19, interest rises to $5,511, and the total is $30,511. With a $10,000 residual, the payment is $355.35, interest is $6,321, and the total is $31,321. Each extra $5,000 of residual saves about $70 a month but costs roughly $810 more in interest.

Residual payment and credit score together

Take the same $25,000 loan over 60 months with a $7,500 residual. At an illustrative 5.5% the payment is about $369 a month and interest is roughly $4,620. At 9.5% it is about $427 a month and $8,110 in interest. At 15.5% it is about $518 a month and $13,570 in interest. The score alone changes the interest by almost $8,950, and a residual does not remove that gap.

Residual and loan term

On the same $25,000 loan at 7% with a $7,500 residual, a 36-month term costs about $584 a month and $3,530 in interest. A 48-month term is about $463 a month and $4,715 in interest. A 60-month term is about $390 a month and $5,916 in interest. A longer term with a residual gives the lowest monthly payment, and also the highest total cost.

Residual value in a car lease

In a lease, the residual value is what the lessor expects the car to be worth at the end, and it is not a payment you make. For a $25,000 car over 36 months with a 7% equivalent rate (money factor 0.002917), a $15,000 residual gives about $394 a month, an $18,000 residual about $320, and a $21,000 residual about $245. The higher the residual, the less depreciation you pay for. A lease is a different product from a loan, so this calculator does not replace one.

Frequently asked questions

What is a residual (balloon) payment on a car loan?

A lump sum due at the end, usually near the car's expected value then. It lowers the monthly payment, but you must pay, refinance, or sell the car when the term ends.

Does a residual payment lower the total cost of the loan?

No. It lowers the monthly payment but increases total interest, because part of the balance stays unpaid throughout the term.

What happens when the loan ends and the residual is due?

You can pay it in cash, refinance it, or sell the car and use the proceeds. If the car is worth less than the residual, you owe the difference.
