Worked example: how credit score changes a $25,000 car loan

Take a $25,000 loan over 60 months. With an excellent score and an illustrative 5.5% rate, the payment is about $478 a month and you pay roughly $3,650 in interest. With a fair score at 9.5%, the same loan costs about $525 a month and $6,500 in interest. At 15.5%, the payment is about $601 a month and interest reaches roughly $11,100. The car and the term are identical; the score alone moves the total cost by more than $7,400.

How your down payment changes the payment

Buying a $30,000 car at 7% over 60 months, a $0 down payment means borrowing $30,000: about $594 a month and $5,640 in interest. Put $3,000 down and the payment drops to about $535. Put $6,000 down (20%) and it falls to about $475 a month with roughly $4,500 in interest. A larger down payment also lowers the risk of owing more than the car is worth in the first years, when new cars lose value fastest.

Loan term: lower payment or lower total cost

On a $25,000 loan at 7%, a 48-month term costs about $599 a month and $3,735 in interest. A 60-month term is about $495 a month and $4,700 in interest. A 72-month term is about $426 a month but $5,690 in interest. A longer term is easier on the monthly budget and more expensive overall, so use the calculator to find the shortest term you can comfortably afford.

Frequently asked questions

How does my credit score affect my car loan payment?

Lenders set your rate largely by your score. A higher score means a lower rate, a lower monthly payment, and much less total interest on the same loan.

How much should I put down on a car?

A common guideline is 10% to 20% of the price. More down means a smaller loan, a lower payment, and less interest.

Is a longer car loan term a good idea?

It lowers the monthly payment but raises total interest and keeps you in debt longer. Compare terms above before you choose.

Are the interest rates in this calculator real offers?

No. They are illustrations for each score range. Type in your own rate at any time to get an exact result.
